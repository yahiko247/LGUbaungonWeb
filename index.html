About Our Municipality

Municipality of Baungon, Bukidnon

Baungon is a 2nd class municipality in the province of Bukidnon, Philippines. Rich in cultural heritage, agriculture, and breathtaking natural landscapes, Baungon celebrated its historic 70th founding anniversary (Araw ng Baungon) in October 2026.

16 Barangays

United under progressive local governance

Vibrant Community

Home to resilient indigenous and migrant settlers

70 Years of Legacy

Commemorating diamond-plus progress and unity

Vision & Mission

To be a premier agro-industrial and ecotourism center in Bukidnon, powered by God-fearing, empowered, and healthy citizenry living in a sustainable and peaceful environment.
